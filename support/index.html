Support

Questions, problems and missing cameras are all welcome. The quickest route is to open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Why am I not getting alerts?

Alerts need three things: location access, a camera database, and driving speed. Check Settings for the location and database warnings on the home screen, and note that Wayside stays quiet below about 11 mph, so it cannot be tested from a desk.

Which cameras are covered?

Fixed speed cameras and red-light cameras in Great Britain, from the community database, refreshed weekly. Mobile camera sites are not included unless your own imported database or reported cameras contain them.

A camera is missing or wrong

Tap Report Camera while driving to record its position, then add the type and speed limit afterwards in Settings. Corrections to the community database itself go to Lufop.net, whose data it is.

Can I use my own camera data?

Yes. Download a CSV export from a commercial camera subscription onto the phone, then share it to Wayside from Files or use Settings › Camera Data › Import.

What does Premium include?

Average speed zones, plus alerts, details and history for the cameras you report, and the Stats page. It is a yearly subscription with a free trial, managed in your Apple Account settings.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Wayside Premium there. Cancelling stops the renewal; Premium stays available until the end of the period you have paid for.

Privacy

Nothing about your journeys leaves your iPhone. The detail is in the privacy policy.
